# Validation scope

The exact replay covers twelve original sparse certificates, fresh rational
fixtures, malformed-input rejection, known-anchor gradients, encoded continuous
piecewise-affine certificates and frozen finite response tables. Checkpoint and
input bindings are recomputed. The recorded focused suite has 48 tests.

Numerical scripts regenerate the original sparse/basis, weighted-gain,
negative-feedback, joint LP, calibrated-field and softplus checks. Their output
hashes are compared with the frozen references. Floating arithmetic remains
numerical evidence rather than outward-rounded interval certification.

The original independent finite-table validation logic is retained. Its slow
pair loop is accelerated with algebraically equivalent common-denominator
integer arithmetic; tests compare errors and witnesses with the Fraction loop.

The checks are conditional on the supplied model-class and observation premises.
They do not independently establish training accuracy, all unselected candidate
scores, public protocol timing, or universal coefficient-extractor correctness.
Finite response nets do not prove continuous-domain recovery. No training or
scientific claims beyond the checkers' acceptance scope are implied.

Only one nonsemantic field in the frozen prospective summary was normalized:
an absolute sidecar path became relative. Numeric values, weights, input arrays
and proof sidecars are unchanged. The original experiment protocol is retained
because its exact digest is an acceptance obligation of the checker.
